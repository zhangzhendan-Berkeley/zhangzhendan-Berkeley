Part 1.8 — Visual Anagrams

Using two prompts and flipping the latent during denoising, the model forms an image that changes its meaning when rotated 180°. Prompts used:

  • Example 1: "an oil painting of people around a campfire" ↔ "an oil painting of an old man"
  • Example 2: "a charcoal portrait of an elderly woman with deep wrinkles" ↔ "a charcoal drawing of a haunted Victorian mansion on a hill"
  • Example 3: "a watercolor painting of a young girl smiling" ↔ "a watercolor of a blooming tree with twisted roots"

Part 1.9 — Hybrid Images

Hybrid images are produced by blending low-frequency components from prompt 1 with high-frequency components from prompt 2. Prompts used:

  • Hybrid 1: "a lithograph of waterfalls" + "a lithograph of a skull"
  • Hybrid 2: "an oil painting of a wise old wizard with a long beard, dramatic lighting" + "an oil painting of a burning forest at night, glowing embers and rising smoke"
  • Hybrid 3: "a minimalist painting of a giant moon over the ocean" + "an intricate ink drawing of a dragon"
